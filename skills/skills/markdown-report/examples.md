# markdown-report — Example

## Sales report

**Input**: Q1 data — revenue $12M (+15% YoY), top region East, North underperforming.

**Output**:
```markdown
# Q1 Sales Report

## Summary
Revenue grew 15% YoY to $12M, driven by East region. North region requires attention.

## Data Overview
| Metric        | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Total Revenue | $12M  |
| YoY Growth    | 15%   |
| Top Region    | East  |

## Analysis
East region contributed 40% of total revenue, outperforming all other regions.
North region declined 8% YoY; pricing and channel mix are likely factors.

## Conclusions & Recommendations
- Increase marketing budget for East region in Q2
- Conduct North region channel audit by end of April
```
